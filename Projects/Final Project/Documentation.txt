 Game Overview
A 2D farming simulator where players have 20 days to grow crops, manage resources, and earn money by selling to a merchant boat.

 How to Play
- Use the Hoe, Water, Plant, and Harvest buttons to manage your farm
- Select different seeds from the toolbar
- Crops only grow during daytime (6 AM - 8 PM)
- A merchant boat arrives at 2 AM - walk near it to sell your harvest
- Goal: Earn as much money as possible in 20 days

 Key Features
- Day/night cycle with dynamic lighting
- Multiple crop types with different growth rates (Pumpkins, Carrots, Strawberries)
- Wild foraging system for blueberries
- Merchant boat trading system with proximity detection
- Inventory management with UI display
- Time and currency tracking
- Game over with final score

 Controls
- Movement: Arrow Keys or WASD
- Actions: UI Buttons (Hoe, Water, Plant, Harvest, Sell)
- Seed Selection: Click seed icons in toolbar

 Main Code 
 
- TimeManager:** Handles day/night cycle and day progression
- CropManager:** Manages farm tile grid and crop growth
- ForagingManager:** Handles wild crop spawning and growth
- InventorySystem:** Tracks collected items and quantities
- CurrencyManager:** Handles money and transactions
- ShopSystem:** Manages selling items to merchant
- MerchantBoatManager:** Controls boat arrival/departure
